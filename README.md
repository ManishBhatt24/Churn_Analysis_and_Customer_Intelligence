# 📊 Churn Analysis and Customer Intelligence

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is a major challenge for businesses because losing customers can reduce revenue and increase customer acquisition costs. This project analyzes customer data to identify churn patterns, understand customer behavior, and discover potential retention risks.

Using Python and data analysis libraries, this project explores customer churn across subscription plans, geographic regions, acquisition channels, and customer support metrics. The findings are used to develop data-driven business recommendations that can help improve customer retention.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall customer churn and retention rates.
- Identify subscription plans with higher churn rates.
- Explore churn patterns across different states and regions.
- Compare customer acquisition channels and their relationship with churn.
- Investigate customer support metrics, complaints, and escalations.
- Visualize important trends and relationships in the data.
- Generate actionable recommendations to improve customer retention.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **Python** – Data analysis and processing
- **Pandas** – Data cleaning, manipulation, and aggregation
- **NumPy** – Numerical computations
- **Matplotlib** – Data visualization
- **Seaborn** – Statistical visualization
- **Jupyter Notebook** – Interactive analysis and documentation

## 🔍 Project Workflow

1. **Data Collection:** Load customer-related data from Excel files.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Inspect data types, missing values, duplicate records, and inconsistent categories.
3. **Data Preparation:** Transform and combine datasets where required for analysis.
4. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Examine churn patterns across customer segments.
5. **Data Visualization:** Create charts, plots, and correlation heatmaps using Matplotlib and Seaborn.
6. **Customer Segmentation:** Compare churn across subscription plans, regions, and acquisition channels.
7. **Business Insights:** Identify customer groups that may require retention-focused attention.
8. **Recommendations:** Suggest strategies based on the observed patterns.

## 📈 Key Areas of Analysis

- Overall churn and retention
- Churn by subscription plan
- State-wise customer churn
- Acquisition channel analysis
- Monthly charges and customer distribution
- Customer tenure and behavior
- Customer complaints and support escalations
- Customer churn risk indicators

## 💡 Business Value

The analysis can help businesses:

- Identify customer segments with relatively high churn rates.
- Prioritize retention campaigns for at-risk customer groups.
- Evaluate subscription plans that may need further investigation.
- Review acquisition channels based on customer retention patterns.
- Investigate whether customer complaints and support experiences are associated with churn.
- Make more informed decisions using data-driven insights.
